The Role

I led field services for a region of higher-education campuses and kept hands-on network responsibility at the same time. My five assigned campuses were my direct, around-the-clock responsibility. Separately, I helped specialists resolve issues across 20+ other campuses, supporting not just residence halls but entire administrative buildings as well. That broader assistance was regional escalation and team support, not an additional 20+ campuses under my direct ownership.

What I Did

  • Led a Distributed Team

    Supervised and developed 14 field service specialists, and covered nearly 30 during staffing vacancies.

  • Owned Direct Support and Assisted Regional Escalations

    Maintained 24/7/365 responsibility for the five assigned campuses and assisted field specialists with switching, wireless, authentication and Layer 1 to 3 escalations across 20+ additional campuses, including residential and administrative facilities.

  • Delivered Refreshes and Deployments

    Participated in and coordinated 10+ network refresh and installation projects across environments with 1,000+ wireless access points and 250 switches.

  • Standardized the Work

    Standardized troubleshooting documentation and wrote Cisco switch firmware-upgrade training materials.

  • Fed Findings Back

    Was selected for a cross-functional product advisory board that turned field findings into service improvements.

Scope and Accountability

Direct responsibility: Five assigned campuses across three states, with 24/7/365 accountability for network support and escalations.

Additional regional reach: Technical assistance and escalation support for specialists covering 20+ other campuses. Those locations included student residence halls and complete administrative buildings. This additional reach is not counted as direct site ownership.

User population: The reported 6,500+ figure describes the users served by the directly assigned campus networks; it is not presented as the total user count across the broader 20+ campus support footprint.
