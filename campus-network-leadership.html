The Role

I led field services for a region of higher-education campuses and kept hands-on network responsibility at the same time. That meant supervising and developing a distributed team while still working switching, wireless and authentication problems myself.

What I Did

  • Led a Distributed Team

  • Owned Escalations

    Coordinated switching, wireless, authentication and Layer 1 to 3 escalations across the groups above.

  • Delivered Refreshes and Deployments

    Supported more than 10 campus-wide network refreshes and more than 10 installations or multi-site deployments.

  • Standardized the Work

    Standardized troubleshooting documentation and wrote Cisco switch firmware-upgrade training materials.

  • Fed Findings Back

    Was selected for a cross-functional product advisory board that turned field findings into service improvements.
