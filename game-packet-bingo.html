The Idea

Discovery is the first step of any network job: what is out there, and how does it connect? Packet Bingo makes that a game, with the card standing in for a network you scan, mark and route across.

How It Plays

  1. ScanDevices, services and events appear
  2. →
  3. MarkClaim matching nodes on your card
  4. →
  5. RouteComplete lines and patterns
  6. →
  7. Level upHarder networks and challenges

Level types include standard, pattern, intersection, blackout, combo and score-attack rounds.
