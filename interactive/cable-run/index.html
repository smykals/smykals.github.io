Work order

Connect your cable to the visible orange exit port by exploring the hidden aisles.

Bonus star goals

Controls

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD
  • Click: Click an adjacent tile to move there
  • Reel cable: Use Reel Cable or press R. Copper reels two tiles; Fiber reels one.
  • Pause: Press Escape or Menu button
  • Run cable from the green switch to the orange exit.
  • Explore lit aisles; discovered rooms stay dimly visible.
Room systems
  • Data packets score points; quick pickups build a streak. Packet routes open from room 2.
  • Rats begin in room 3. A flashing rat is chewing exposed cable; reel in that segment within 2 seconds.
  • Repellent protects from rats for 8 seconds from room 4. Surge panels appear in room 5: cyan is safe, amber warns, red damages cable.
  • Patrol rats join in room 7; ambushers join in room 11.
  • Drill unlocks in room 12. Conduit unlocks in room 14.
  • Copper is vulnerable on warning or active surge panels. Fiber ignores surges; the meter warns at 4 bends in 8 steps and damages integrity at 5.
  • Collect 60% of packets, protect full integrity, or finish within 125% of the shortest route for bonus stars.
