How to connect.

  1. Start the scanner. It announces a name and symbol.
  2. Find the called number on your card and click it. Some calls may not be on your card.
  3. Connect five across, down, or diagonally. LOCALHOST is already connected.

Earlier calls remain valid. Pause whenever you like, or turn off Auto scan and use Next scan. Use Tab and Enter to select nodes.

Meet every objective shown above the card. Scores carry between levels. Chains unlock at Level 2, multipliers at 3, and power daubs at 4. Powered squares still require their number to be called. Wrong clicks break your combo and multiplier. Later milestone challenges have a timer; other levels end after all calls and eligible daubs are used. Next level deals a fresh card.
