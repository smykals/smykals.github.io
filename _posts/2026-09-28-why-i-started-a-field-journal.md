A project page shows the finished system. A field journal captures the decisions, tests, setbacks, and changes that got it there.

I work across field services, infrastructure, systems, and networking. My independent lab gives me room to build and break things, trace a problem from the endpoint through the network and service, and document what actually resolved it.

Here I will write about projects in progress as well as completed work: the objective, the environment in broad terms, the steps I took, what the evidence showed, and what I would improve next time. Expect entries about network segmentation, virtualization, local AI services, and the operational details that matter when a design meets reality.

Configurations and addresses will be sanitized. The point is to make the reasoning reproducible without exposing private network details.

If you are reviewing my portfolio, [the featured projects]({{ '/' | relative_url }}#projects) provide the overview. This journal adds the working notes behind them.
