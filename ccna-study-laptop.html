Build Overview / Study Workstation

CCNA Study Laptop

A repurposed Dell Latitude 5480 running Parrot OS: a portable workbench for Cisco labs, Linux diagnostics, device management, and packet analysis.

Contribution and supporting artifacts

I repurposed the laptop and documented the workstation build. Tool availability and learning plans are distinct from proficiency demonstrated through completed exercises.

01 / Build & Purpose

From an unused laptop to a networking workbench.

I had an older Dell laptop that still worked but no longer had a clear purpose. Rather than putting Packet Tracer on my everyday computer, I wiped the laptop, installed Parrot OS, and dedicated it to networking study and experimentation.

Keeping this environment separate lets me try unfamiliar tools, change configurations, and rebuild when needed. The value is the range of tasks one portable machine can support.

03 / Physical & Virtual Lab Use

One machine, several perspectives.

Endpoint & test clientJoin the physical Cisco lab and inspect what a connected system actually experiences.
Management terminalAccess network equipment and compare device state with endpoint observations.
Packet analyzerCapture traffic to investigate what is happening on the wire.
Virtual lab platformUse Packet Tracer to practice Cisco networking away from the physical equipment.

The guiding question is what each observation proves. A command becomes useful when I know why I am running it, what I expect to see, and how its output narrows the investigation.

04 / Continuing Development

Extend the workbench as the exercises require.

Python scripting, Git-based management of scripts and configurations, lightweight services, and containers are directions for extending this environment. They are not presented here as completed automation or deployed services.

The immediate focus remains CCNA practice and deeper understanding of the protocols behind the commands.
