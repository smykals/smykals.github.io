Packet Route

An arcade strategy game: aim and fire packets to match traffic, clear the network, and stop it from saturating as new traffic keeps arriving.

The Idea

Congestion is what happens when traffic arrives faster than the network clears it. Packet Route lets you feel that: match traffic to clear it, or watch the network saturate.

How It Plays

  1. AimLine up the shot
  2. →
  3. FireSend the packet
  4. →
  5. MatchMatching traffic clears
  6. →
  7. New trafficRows keep arriving
  8. →
  9. Clear or saturateFinish the level or the network fills

Levels: First Connection, Branch Office, Traffic Exchange, Core Switch, Storage Fabric, Full Spectrum.

Game states

Splitting the engine, levels and renderer kept each part small and testable.
