From Smart Home Professional to Team Trainer

At Vivint, I progressed from installing and troubleshooting connected security systems to training technicians as Team Trainer. I trained six technicians and cut my own repeat service calls by 30%.

The Problem

A repeat service call means the first visit didn't fix the problem. It costs the customer a second appointment and the team a second trip. I focused on getting it right the first time in my own work, and on passing that approach to the technicians I trained.

Technical Foundation

Before becoming a Team Trainer, I installed, configured, serviced and troubleshot electronic security and smart-home systems, including control panels, cameras, sensors, smart locks, thermostats and connected automation. I also helped customers understand how their systems worked. I held an Alabama Electronic Security Board of Licensure (AESBL) credential during this employment; this is not a claim of current active licensure.

Results and Measurement Context

The 30% reduction is my own repeat-call rate over my time at Vivint, counted as repeat tickets opened by the same customer. The underlying counts are not published here.

I ranked in the top three of about 100 technicians in my region for one quarter, based on customer ratings, installs and quality scores, and consistently placed in the top five on my team.
