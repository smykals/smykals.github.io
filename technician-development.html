Field Technician Development

As team trainer, I coached 10 to 14 field technicians, and repeat service calls fell by 30%.

Team
10 to 14 field technicians
Field
Smart home technology and field services
Role
Team Trainer
Result
30% fewer repeat service calls

The Problem

A repeat service call means the first visit didn't fix the problem. It costs the customer a second appointment and the team a second trip. Most repeats came back to diagnosis and installation quality, which are skills that can be taught.

The Improvement Loop

  1. TrainDiagnostics, cabling, networking, device integration
  2. →
  3. Field workTechnicians apply it on real installs
  4. →
  5. EscalationI support the hard cases directly
  6. →
  7. Root causeWhy did this one come back?
  8. →
  9. StandardRaise the quality bar for everyone

Each repeat call fed back into training.

What I Did

  • Coached and Developed Technicians

    Supervised, coached and developed 10 to 14 field technicians, with a focus on diagnostic skills, installation quality and independent problem-solving.

  • Taught Hands-on Skills

    Delivered training in diagnostics, structured cabling, networking and device integration.

  • Stayed the Escalation Point

    Remained a technical escalation resource for complex field issues.

  • Raised Field Standards

    Used root-cause troubleshooting to improve field quality standards.
