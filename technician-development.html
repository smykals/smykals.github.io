Leadership case study, 2016 to 2018

From Smart Home Professional to Team Trainer

At Vivint, I progressed from installing and troubleshooting connected security systems to coaching 10–14 technicians. That work contributed to a reported 30% reduction in regional repeat service calls.

Team

The Problem

A repeat service call means the first visit didn't fix the problem. It costs the customer a second appointment and the team a second trip. The improvement effort focused on more consistent diagnosis, installation quality and technician problem-solving.

Technical Foundation

Before becoming a Team Trainer, I installed, configured, serviced and troubleshot electronic security and smart-home systems, including control panels, cameras, sensors, smart locks, thermostats and connected automation. I also helped customers understand how their systems worked. I held an Alabama Electronic Security Board of Licensure (AESBL) credential during this employment; this is not a claim of current active licensure.

Results and Measurement Context

The 30% reduction is a reported career outcome. The underlying callback counts, baseline and comparison windows, calculation method and individual attribution are not available for independent verification on this page. It should not be read as a controlled study or a result achieved alone.

I was also ranked among the top three Smart Home Professionals nationally against established performance KPIs. This is a reported historical achievement; the original scorecard, measurement window and KPI weighting are not published here. The 10–14 technician figure describes coaching scope rather than a productivity measurement.
