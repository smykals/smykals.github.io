About Me

Technical by nature.
Always learning.

I’m an IT professional with 10 years of IT experience and 13 years in field services. My career has consistently progressed toward greater technical and leadership responsibility, with each of my previous organizations providing opportunities to move into leadership roles.

My background combines hands-on troubleshooting, technical operations, infrastructure support, root-cause analysis, process improvement, and people development. I’ve supported enterprise and manufacturing environments while continuously expanding my technical depth through hands-on work, independent projects, and ongoing study.

I’m a continuous learner who isn’t afraid to take on a challenge or step into unfamiliar territory. I don’t need to know everything on day one. I’m willing to dig in, learn what I need to learn, and work through a problem until I understand it.

I approach technology by asking why — why a system failed, why an issue keeps returning, and what can be changed to prevent it from happening again. That same approach carries into leadership: understand the problem, understand the people involved, and improve the system rather than simply address the immediate symptom.
